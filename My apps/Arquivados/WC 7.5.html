ℹ️ How Auto-Draw Works

The system prioritizes Max limits per tag over the Acts per draw setting. If your tag limits are reached and there are no untagged activities left in the bank, the draw will stop and provide fewer activities than requested. Untagged activities bypass limits and act as unrestricted fillers.
